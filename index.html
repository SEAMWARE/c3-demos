Madrid (CDMAD)

A smart city: Madrid's bus lines, streetlights, air quality, weather and traffic - a page that moves the world, c³ following with its alarms. In c³, type CDMAD as the broker.

Running one

Needs coraine, curl, and c³ built and running - checked out beside this repository, whose launchers use its example scripts:

git clone https://github.com/SEAMWARE/c3-demos     # beside c3/
cd c3-demos
factory/launch.sh                                    # or bikes/launch.sh

It starts a coraine of its own in RAM, loads the demo and says where to connect: in c³, Explore, enter http://localhost:1090 (bikes: 1092) and press Connect. Ctrl-C stops it. More in the README.
